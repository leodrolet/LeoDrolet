Frequently asked questions.

Another question? Write to me, I reply within 24 hours.

What does the monthly plan include?

Building your website, hosting it, managing your domain name, security and backups, maintenance and updates, search engine optimization, a working contact form, traffic statistics and technical support. Monthly editing hours are also included: 1 hour with Growth, up to 2 hours with Premium. A one-time $200 setup fee is added to the first month.

Can I ask for changes to my website?

Yes. Every month, your plan includes hours for content or design edits: 1 hour with Growth, up to 2 hours with Premium. Send me what you'd like to change (text, photos, sections) and I take care of it.

What happens if I go over my monthly editing hours?

I let you know before going over the hours included in your plan. Any extra work is then offered to you with a clear estimate, and nothing is done without your approval.

Do I own my domain name?

Yes. Your domain name stays in your name: it belongs to you. I simply handle its technical management as part of your plan.

Can I cancel my plan?

Yes. Cancellation terms are spelled out clearly in your service agreement, before the project starts. You know exactly what to expect from day one.

How long does it take to build my website?

Usually 2 to 3 weeks between the first call and launch. The exact timeline depends on the number of pages and how quickly your content (text, photos, logo) comes together. I give you a precise timeline on our first call.

Do you work remotely or in person?

Both. The first call can be a video call or an in-person meeting in Gatineau or Ottawa. The work is then done remotely, with previews to approve at every step.
